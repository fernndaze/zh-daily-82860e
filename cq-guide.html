Sound shifts — standard tape vs. Chongqing street

Approximate; sources in research/chongqing/. Your dialect cards show both spellings — these are the systematic moves underneath them.

Tones

Chongqing tone contours run roughly: high level-ish (阴平 ~45), low fall (阳平 ~21), falling (上声 ~42), dipping (去声 ~213). Same tone categories as standard, different shapes — that's most of the 'accent'.

Two systems that look alike

Chongqing's own 儿 endings (娃儿 · 幺儿 · 哈哈儿 · 一滴滴儿) are real light syllables — a Southwest system, not Beijing's fused ending. Your cards keep these and ban the fused one.

The -tóu locative family: 屋头 at home · 心头 in the mind · 手头 on hand · 外头 outside — Chongqing's default where standard says 家里 / 心里 / 身上 / 外面.

Register tiers (the ⚠️ rule)

SAFE — say freely, including with in-laws.

TEASE ⚠️ — affectionate mockery between close people: 瓜娃子, 瓜兮兮, 宝器. Tone decides; 宝器 runs harsher in Chongqing than in Chengdu.

VULGAR — never taught on this site (the street retorts you'll hear anyway).

Northern forms you'll hear on TV — passive recognition only

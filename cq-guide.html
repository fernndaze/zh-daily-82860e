Chongqing is not Chengdu

méidéstdCQ

Chongqing's 'don't have' — Chengdu says mòdé. Produce méidé.

standard: méiyǒu

You'll hear both on TV, but they are different targets. Chongqing: fast, steep, clipped — méidé (not Chengdu mòdé), lānggè (not zǎgè), hēi (not hěn), lè diǎn (not zhè guō). Chongqing also merges n into l COMPLETELY, where Chengdu keeps a nasal before i/ü sounds.

If a 'Sichuanese' lesson teaches zǎgè or mòdé as your production forms, it is teaching you Chengdu.
